‎Yolat   Web3 Africa Discovery
‎
‎Overview
‎
‎Yolat is a financial technology platform focused on simplifying cross-border payments and financial services.
‎
‎I selected Yolat as part of my Web3 Africa Discovery research because it sits at an interesting intersection between fintech, cross-border payments, and digital assets.
‎
‎This research documents what I learned while exploring the project from a beginner's perspective.
‎
‎
‎The Problem
‎
‎Cross-border payments can be complicated because of:
‎
· ‎Multiple currencies
· ‎Exchange-rate differences
· ‎Transaction fees
· ‎Payment intermediaries
· ‎Delays in international transfers
· ‎Limited access to global financial services
‎
‎These challenges can affect individuals, freelancers, remote workers, and businesses operating across different countries.
‎
‎
‎The Web3 Connection
‎
‎One of the areas that caught my attention was Yolat's support for digital assets and stablecoins.
‎
‎Stablecoins such as USDT and USDC are designed to maintain a value linked to the US dollar.
‎
‎This creates an interesting connection between:
‎
‎Digital assets → Payment infrastructure → Fiat currencies → Real-world financial use
‎
‎Researching Yolat helped me understand that Web3 applications are not limited to cryptocurrency trading. Blockchain-based assets can also play a role in payments and financial infrastructure.
‎
‎
‎African Use Case
‎
‎Cross-border financial activity is particularly important in Africa, where businesses, freelancers, families, and remote workers frequently interact across different currencies and financial systems.
‎
‎This made Yolat interesting to me because its focus is not simply on digital assets, but on how financial services can work across borders.
‎
‎
‎What I Learned
‎
‎Researching Yolat helped me understand several concepts more clearly:
‎
1. ‎Stablecoins can have practical applications beyond trading.
2. ‎Cross-border payments are an important area for fintech innovation.
3. ‎Web3 can interact with traditional financial infrastructure rather than necessarily replacing it.
4. ‎African fintech and Web3 projects are addressing real-world financial problems.
5. ‎Understanding the problem a technology solves is just as important as understanding the technology itself.
‎
